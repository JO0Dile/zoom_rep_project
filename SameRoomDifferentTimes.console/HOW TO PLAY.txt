SAME ROOM, DIFFERENT TIMES (test build, 15 October 2026)

1. Unzip this folder anywhere. Keep all the files together.
2. Double-click SameRoomDifferentTimes.exe.

Two players, one in 1987 and one in 2053:
- On Steam (best over the internet): both have Steam open. The host picks Host a game, chooses a time, presses
  "Host on Steam" and invites the friend from the Steam window that opens. The friend accepts the invite and the
  game joins by itself. Steam will show the game as "Spacewar": that is Valve's free test app for games in
  development, nothing else.
- Same house or Wi-Fi: Host a game, Start hosting; the friend picks Join a friend and types the address shown.

Keys: WASD move, mouse look, E use, Q throw, 1-7 belt, I bag, R notebook, Ctrl crouch, Shift run, V talk (walkie),
K emotes, Esc menu. In BuzzKiller's 4D room: Z and X move through the fourth direction (only on the glowing plates).
